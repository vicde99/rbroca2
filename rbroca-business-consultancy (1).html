FAQ

Pricing questions

Is the price really fixed?

Yes. Each retainer is a flat monthly fee agreed in your proposal. We only revisit it if your scope changes, such as more employees or a new branch.

What if I just need a one-off filing or consultation?

Your first 30-minute consultation is free, and one-off filings and add-on work are quoted per scope without a retainer.

Do you charge setup fees?

Any onboarding or cleanup work is listed as a line item in your proposal before you sign, so there are no surprise charges.

Can I switch tiers mid-year?

Yes. Move up or down when your business changes, and we adjust the monthly fee from the next billing cycle.

What's not included in any tier?

Government fees and taxes, third-party software subscriptions, and work outside the agreed scope are billed separately.
